What we know about you: almost nothing

This site has no accounts, no ads, no tracking cookies and no comments. We don't collect your name or email address unless you choose to send them to us, and we never sell or share anything.

Visit counting

{% if goatcounter %}

We count visits with GoatCounter, so we know which pages people read. It sets no cookies and doesn't store your IP address or anything else that identifies you; it keeps the page, where you came from, and your browser, screen size and country.

{% else %}

We don't count visits at the moment.

{% endif %}

The companies that serve the site

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses to keep the service running and secure. The fonts come from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when it loads them. Their own privacy statements apply.

Stored in your browser

If you press the light/dark button, your browser remembers your choice. It stays on your device and we never see it.

{% if newsletter_url %}

The newsletter

If you sign up, the newsletter service holds your email address under its own privacy policy, and we use it only to send the newsletter. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

{% endif %}

If you contact us

We use what you send only to reply, and delete it when it's no longer needed. Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete anything we hold about you. Contact us to do that, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office or your local regulator.
