How it works, and what it can't see

Each remaining game is simulated from both teams' attack and defence ratings, the same ones behind our weekly calls, using results up to {{ sim.as_of }}. Every run gives a full final table. The ratings are held fixed for the whole season, so the projection can't foresee injuries, new managers or January signings. Real uncertainty is wider than these numbers suggest, especially months out. It's a picture of where each club stands today, not a promise.
